ACMEPAY ENGINEERING INCIDENT RESPONSE PROCESS
Severity 1: Confirmed widespread payment outage, suspected compromise of sensitive payment systems, or ledger integrity concern.
Severity 2: Material degradation affecting multiple merchants without confirmed data compromise.
Severity 3: Limited functionality issue with a workaround.

Roles:
Incident Commander coordinates response and decisions.
Technical Lead directs investigation and remediation.
Communications Lead manages stakeholder updates.
Scribe maintains timeline and decisions.

First actions:
1. Acknowledge the alert and create an incident channel.
2. Assign an Incident Commander.
3. Establish scope and customer impact.
4. Preserve relevant logs and evidence.
5. Mitigate safely; do not destroy forensic evidence.
6. Provide updates at the cadence set by the Incident Commander.

After resolution:
Complete a blameless review within five business days. Record root causes, contributing factors, detection gaps, and tracked corrective actions.
