# Refund and Reversal Guidance

**Owner:** Payments Operations  
**Classification:** Internal

## Reversal
A reversal cancels or releases an authorization before settlement, where supported by the partner. The availability and timing depend on the payment network and partner.

## Refund
A refund is a separate transaction initiated after a payment has been captured. Refunds must reference the original captured payment.

## Support checks
1. Confirm the original payment status.
2. Check whether the payment was captured or only authorized.
3. Confirm that no refund is already in progress.
4. Verify the merchant's permissions.
5. Escalate if ledger records do not reconcile.

## Important
Never promise an exact bank posting date. Issuer processing times vary. Do not create a second refund to resolve a delayed first refund without checking its state.
