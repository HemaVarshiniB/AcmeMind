ACMEPAY WORK-FROM-HOME POLICY
Version: 1.0 | Owner: People Operations | Effective: 2026-01-01

Purpose
This policy defines expectations for remote and hybrid work at AcmePay.

Eligibility
Employees may work remotely when their role, customer commitments, and data-handling responsibilities permit it. Managers confirm team coverage and role eligibility.

Security requirements
Employees must use company-managed devices, approved VPN access, multi-factor authentication, and screen locking. Confidential documents must not be printed or stored on shared household devices. Public Wi-Fi may only be used with the approved secure connection.

Availability
Employees should maintain their agreed team working hours and keep their calendar status current. Managers may establish team-specific collaboration windows.

Equipment
Company-issued equipment remains company property. Lost or stolen devices must be reported to Security Operations immediately.

Exceptions
Exceptions require written approval from the department head and Security Operations.
