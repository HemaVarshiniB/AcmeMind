REMOTE WORK FAQ

Q: Can I work from another country?
A: Not automatically. International remote work requires prior approval from People Operations, Legal, Tax, and Information Security because employment, tax, and data-transfer rules may apply.

Q: Can I use my personal laptop?
A: No. Company systems containing internal or customer information must be accessed from an approved, managed device.

Q: What if my internet connection fails?
A: Notify your manager through an approved alternate channel and follow the team's continuity plan.

Q: Who approves a long-term remote arrangement?
A: The department head approves the business arrangement after People Operations and Security review.

Q: Where do I report a lost laptop?
A: Contact Security Operations immediately using the incident hotline listed in the internal directory.
